Sign in

or with a username and password

Directory (LDAP / Active Directory) accounts sign in here with their usual username and password.

Password sign-in for local accounts is limited to administrators.

New installation: sign in as aresadmin with the one-time password in initial-admin-password.txt in the data directory (DATA_DIR). After choosing a new password and setting up two-factor authentication you can restore an earlier installation from Administration → Backup & Restore.

Two-factor authentication

Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app, or one of your recovery codes (xxxx-xxxx).

Choose a new password

Your password must be changed before you continue. At least 12 characters, with three of: lowercase, uppercase, digits, symbols.

Set up two-factor authentication

Your administrator requires it. Scan the code with an authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, FreeOTP…), or type the key in, then enter the 6-digit code it shows.

Save your recovery codes

Each code signs you in once if you lose your phone. They are shown only now: keep them somewhere safe, away from this computer.
